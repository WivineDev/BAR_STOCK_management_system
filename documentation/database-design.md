# Database design (v0.1)

The importable schema is [`backend/database/bssbms.sql`](../backend/database/bssbms.sql), followed by the versioned migrations under `backend/database/migrations/`. It uses InnoDB, UUID string keys, foreign keys, tenant-aware indexes, DECIMAL monetary and stock values, and append-only sales, purchase, return, payment, movement, and audit records.

Core relationships: businesses own branches, users, customers, categories, products, sales, purchases, expenses, sessions, and audit records. Products may belong to one business category, own one branch inventory balance, and have many stock movement entries. A sale owns immutable line snapshots and payments; sales returns refer to those original sale items and may include refund payments. Purchases own immutable received item snapshots; purchase returns refer to those purchase items and reduce the purchase balance or collect an immediate supplier refund. Customer and supplier payments allocate across outstanding sales and purchases. Cash sessions own their cash movements.

Return quantities and values are stored separately from the original transaction snapshots. A sales return applies first to `sales.balance_due`; only the remainder can be refunded, and refunds cannot exceed payments collected (direct sale payments plus customer payment allocations, less prior refunds). A purchase return applies first to `purchases.balance_due`; any remainder must be received as a supplier refund during the return. Returned items are restricted to quantities still available on the original line; supplier returns also require enough current inventory. Sale lines snapshot the product's category name so later product recategorization does not rewrite historical category reports. Additional role-permission, attachment, and reporting snapshot tables remain future work.
