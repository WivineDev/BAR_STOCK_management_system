# User guide (implemented workflows)

1. Create the first owner account from the sign-in page.
2. Create product categories from Categories, then add a product with its category, base unit, sale price, cost, and low-stock threshold. The low-stock threshold can be zero.
3. Use the product's Units action to define packages such as `1 crate = 20 bottles`, with separate sale and purchase prices. Conversions can be edited later from Settings → Product Units & Conversions; the base unit is fixed forever and already-recorded transactions keep their original snapshot, so changing a factor only affects future purchases and sales.
4. Add suppliers, then record a received purchase. Choose its package quantity and unit cost; the backend adds converted base stock and recalculates weighted-average cost. Partial supplier payments leave an outstanding balance.
5. Open Inventory and record an opening count with a reason.
6. Open Cash Management and enter the till float before accepting cash.
7. In Point of Sale, choose the channel at the top: **Walk-in** for instant counter checkout, or **Table service** to bill a seated table. For walk-ins, search by product name or barcode (or press Enter to add an exact barcode match), pick the selling unit on the product card, and adjust quantities with the +/- controls or by typing in the quantity box. Line subtotals, the bill total, and payment balance update automatically. Owners and managers can apply a discount (a flat amount or quick 5%/10%); cashiers need their approval. Select one or split payment methods, then complete the sale — stock is reduced automatically and a receipt opens for printing or downloading. A credit remainder requires an identified customer. A cart in progress can be held with a label and resumed later; holding never reserves or changes stock.
8. Configure the floor once from Settings → Floor & Seating (owners and managers): create areas such as Main Hall or Terrace, then add numbered tables with capacity and position. Tables can be renamed, moved between areas, or taken out of service; a table referenced by past bills is deactivated rather than deleted so history stays intact.
9. For table service: open the Floor page and pick a free table — customers are anonymous, no name is needed. Optionally assign a waiter. In Point of Sale table mode, record rounds: save them as drafts while the customers decide (drafts do not touch stock), then confirm serving — stock is deducted exactly once per served round. Mistaken rounds can be voided with a reason; a voided served round returns its stock. Split the bill across cash, mobile money, card, or bank transfer, then close the table: the bill is saved as one sale with the table code on the receipt, and the table becomes free again. If the customers leave an unpaid balance, an owner or manager can close the table onto a registered customer's credit account and collect it later from Customers & credit — open tabs never mix with customer debt. A whole session can be transferred to another free table, or two tables merged, without losing round history.
10. Record operating expenses. Cash expenses require an open till session.
11. At close, count cash; if counted and expected values differ, enter an explanation.
12. To return a sale, open its Details from Sales and choose Start return. Enter only quantities actually returned and identify whether each item can go back into saleable stock. The system applies the return to customer debt first; when money is due back, select the refund method. A cash refund requires an open cash session.
13. To return stock to a supplier, open the purchase Details and choose Start return. Enter the physical quantity being sent back. The system checks remaining received quantity and stock on hand, reduces the supplier payable first, and requires an immediate supplier refund receipt for any amount above the remaining payable.
14. Owners and managers can archive or restore categories. Editing a product can change its name, SKU, category, selling price, and threshold. The base unit stays fixed and the purchase cost continues to follow weighted-average stock costing.
15. Owners, managers, and accountants can read the table-service section of Reports: open tabs with their live balances, sales by table and area, waiter performance, and average seated time per table.

Implemented workflows cover first-owner setup, owner-managed staff accounts, products, categories, and packaging, suppliers and purchases, purchase and sales returns, inventory adjustments, dual-channel POS with held carts, table service with anonymous tabs, drafts, rounds, split payments, transfers and merges, waiter assignment, sales history and printable receipts, customer credit and payments, expenses, cash sessions, dashboard metrics, category and return-adjusted report summary/CSV/print with table reports, and audit viewing. Granular permission configuration, full settings, customer and supplier statements, and notification workflows are not available yet.
